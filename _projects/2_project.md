Joint work with **Airbus**.

Applied Bayesian learning to Quantum Orthogonal Neural Networks for segmenting faults in 3D car parts, comparing feedforward and 3D convolutional architectures against their Bayesian counterparts to find the optimal solution, with real hardware experiments to benchmark performance {% cite mathur2025bayesian %}.

A related effort designed quantum autoencoders built from orthogonal neural networks to detect anomalies in satellite images of ship routes.
