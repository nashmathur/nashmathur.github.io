Joint work with **F. Hoffmann-La Roche AG**.

Developed both a quantum analogue and a quantum-native variant of the Vision Transformer architecture, and used them to classify Medical MNIST images. The quantum-native variant replaces the self-attention mechanism with orthogonal quantum circuits, and the approach was benchmarked with real hardware experiments {% cite kerenidis2024quantum %}.
