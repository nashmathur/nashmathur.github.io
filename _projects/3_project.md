Joint work with **Harvard Medical School** and **F. Hoffmann-La Roche AG**, as part of the [Wellcome Leap: Quantum for Bio Challenge — Phase 1](https://wellcomeleap.org).

Applied various quantum machine learning techniques to improve MRI acquisition, exploring how near-term quantum models can accelerate imaging pipelines and improve screening quality.
